## Digital Balance Initiative — Volunteer Digital Awareness Campaign ##

**Project Overview**

Digital Balance was a student-led volunteer initiative aimed at helping teenagers develop healthier relationships with technology. Through our research, we realized that the problem was not technology itself, but the difficulty of maintaining control over how we use it. Instead of simply warning teenagers about excessive screen time, we wanted to help them understand their digital habits and take practical steps toward a healthier balance.
Our campaign was built around one message: "Use Technology, Don't Let It Use You."

**What We Created**

We developed an awareness campaign that combined educational materials with an interactive website designed to help teenagers reflect on and manage their digital habits.
The website included:
Digital Habits Assessment: A self-assessment test that helps users evaluate their technology-use habits.
Personalized Recommendations: Suggestions based on each user's assessment results.
Seven-Day Challenge: A structured challenge encouraging users to practice healthier digital habits through small daily steps.
Progress Review: A feature that allows users to review their progress throughout the challenge.
Alongside the website, we created a campaign identity and supporting awareness materials, including a logo, an educational video, social media posts, and presentation slides. Together, these elements were designed to make the topic more relatable and turn general awareness into practical steps teenagers could try.
